My Story

My name is Adrian Nyakundi. I didn't start as a photographer—I started as someone who simply noticed things. The way light slipped through a window, the quiet expression on a stranger's face, the small details others often overlooked.Over time curiosty turned into passion.Photography soon bacame my passion.Through my lens, I found beauty in the ordinary and meaning in fleeting moments. Today, I don't just take pictures, I tell stories, capture emotions, and freeze time in a way that allows others to feel what I felt the exact moment.
